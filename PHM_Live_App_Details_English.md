## 🔷 About PHM Live

**PHM Live** is an all-in-one live streaming and social entertainment platform where users can enjoy live streaming, short videos, random video calls, voice chat rooms, and gaming — all in a single app.

Our mission is to connect people easily, help them make new friends, and build a safe and profitable platform for content creators. On PHM Live, anyone can go live to showcase their talent, receive virtual gifts from viewers, and convert those gifts into real earnings.

**PHM Live — Connect. Stream. Earn.**

## 🔷 Core Features (In Detail)

### 🎥 Live Streaming System
- **Single Live Streaming** — Users can start their own solo live broadcast
- **Multi Live (Group Live)** — Multiple users can join and stream together in one session
- **PK Battle** — Real-time competition between two streamers, with winners decided by gift points
- **Live Chat & Comments** — Viewers can comment and react during live sessions
- **Follow System** — Users can follow their favorite hosts and get notified when they go live

### 📹 Video Calling System
- **Random Video Call** — Connect with strangers through random matching for video calls
- **1-to-1 Video Call** — Direct private video calls with a chosen person
- **Gender/Interest-Based Matching** — Filter matches based on preferences
- **Call Charging System** — Coin deduction per minute or per call

### 🎙️ Voice Chat Rooms
- Create and join multi-user voice rooms
- Host/Co-host management controls
- Send virtual gifts within voice rooms as well

### 🎬 Short Video Feed
- TikTok-style vertical short video uploading and browsing
- Like, comment, share, and follow system
- Trending and "For You" personalized feed

### 🎮 Gaming Zone
- Play mini-games directly within the app
- Earn points/coins by playing games
- Leaderboard system for competitive ranking

### 💎 Virtual Economy
- **Diamond & Coin Wallet** — Virtual currency for sending and receiving gifts
- **Gift Store** — A variety of themed animated virtual gifts
- **Withdraw System** — Hosts can convert earned diamonds into real money
- **Recharge System** — Purchase coins via mobile banking/payment gateways

### 👑 VIP & Membership
- VIP badge and exclusive profile frames
- Access to exclusive gifts and premium features
- Weekly/monthly subscription plans

### 👥 Referral & Agency System
- Earn bonuses by inviting new users through referral links
- Agency panel to manage multiple hosts
- Host performance reports and commission tracking
